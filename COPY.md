# WS outreach copy

Voice: like a person texting a store owner. Lowercase-ish is fine, contractions always, short
sentences, no hype, no bullet walls, no em or en dashes, at most one question per email, no
valedictions ("Best regards"). Caps: opener 90 words, reply 120, follow-up 45, last follow-up 25.
Sign with the sending inbox's first name: `{sender}` (Phil or Farooq).
`{call_with}` = "me" from a Farooq inbox, "Farooq, our founder" from a Phil inbox.
Never quote a performance number (ROAS, CTR, sales). Never promise a refund.
Our ads are "winning ads". Rotate naturally: winning ads, winning ad templates, winning ad styles
researched from your competitors, winning ads researched from what's working, ad styles proven to
lift ROAS. Qualitative only, never with a number.

## Variables
`{inline_ad_previews}` = the 5 ads shown in the email body as small preview images (about 280px wide, JPG), each linking to its full-size file. No attachments unless the inline test fails.
`{first}` first name (fallback "there"), `{store}` company name or domain, `{product}` best-seller
title (short form, no variant text), `{insight}` one true sentence from a fresh Ad Library check,
`{gift_link}`, `{cal}` = https://cal.com/farooq-magicfit/30min, `{code}` = BFCM10.

## Insight sentences (re-check within 72 h of sending; drop the lead if none is still true)
- statics_low: "only {img} of your {total} newest ads are statics"
- oldest: "one of your ads has been live since {month}"
- no_new: "you haven't launched a new ad in the last 30 days"
- new30: "you've launched {new30} new ads in the last 30 days, so you're clearly testing"
- statics_heavy: "{img} of your {total} newest ads are statics"
Subject line = short form of the same fact, lowercase, under 45 characters
(e.g. "4 statics in 30 live ads", "your march ad is still running").
